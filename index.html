Google Calendar

Connect your Google account to display calendars you can access. Your events are read-only.

Create a Web application OAuth client in Google Cloud, enable Google Calendar API, and add this app's website address as an authorized JavaScript origin. See SETUP.md.

iCloud calendars

To include iCloud calendars, subscribe to their read-only calendar links in Google Calendar. They'll then appear in the calendar list above.

Google Calendar on the web → Other calendars → From URL → paste your iCloud public calendar link. For private calendars, use Apple's sharing options carefully; a public calendar URL can be viewed by anyone who has it.
